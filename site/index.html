How it works

Four questions, asked of every investor

The same four things an experienced fundraiser checks first. Each gets a score; together they make one match score.

40%

Do they back your field?

Health specialists who name your area (diagnostics, devices, digital health…) score highest.

25%

Do they invest at your stage?

A Series B fund won't lead your pre-seed. Exact stage scores full, the stage next to it half.

20%

Does their cheque fit your round?

Their usual ticket has to fit inside what you're raising, not dwarf it.

15%

Do they invest where you are?

Local investors first, then Europe. Regional funds that only back one state are flagged.
